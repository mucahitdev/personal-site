# How to Search Text Inside Your Photos on iPhone (Screenshots & Receipts)

**Quick answer:** To find a photo by the words inside it, use an app with on-device text recognition (OCR) that reads and indexes your screenshots, receipts, and notes. [FaceIdent](/faceident) lets you type a word you remember — a store name, a code, a title — and pulls up the matching photo. Everything is processed on your iPhone; nothing is uploaded.

Half of a modern photo library is screenshots, receipts, tickets, and whiteboards. The fastest way to find one is to search the words it contains.

---

## How text search in photos works

Text recognition (OCR) reads the words printed inside an image — a sign, a screenshot, a receipt — and turns them into searchable text. Once your library is indexed, typing a word finds every photo that contains it, even if the file has no caption or filename.

[FaceIdent](/faceident) runs this recognition on-device, so your screenshots and receipts are never uploaded.

## Step-by-step: search text in your photos

1. **Install a photo text-search app.** Download [FaceIdent](/faceident) from the App Store and allow access to your photos.
2. **Let it read on-device.** The app recognizes text in your screenshots, receipts, and notes and indexes it on your iPhone.
3. **Search a word or phrase.** Type something you remember seeing — a brand, an address, a confirmation code.
4. **Open the result.** Tap the matching photo, then copy or share the text.
