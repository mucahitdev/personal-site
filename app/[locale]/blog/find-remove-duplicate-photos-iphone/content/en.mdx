# How to Find and Remove Duplicate Photos on iPhone (Free Up Storage)

**Quick answer:** To clear duplicate photos on iPhone, use an app that scans your library and groups identical and near-identical shots so you can keep the best one and delete the rest. [FaceIdent](/faceident) does this on-device — nothing is uploaded — and removed photos go to Recently Deleted for 30 days, so it is safe to review and clean up.

Burst shots, retries, and saved copies quietly eat gigabytes. Grouping them makes the cleanup a few taps instead of an afternoon.

---

## Why duplicates pile up

Every time you take three shots to get one good one, save a photo twice, or keep a screenshot of a screenshot, you add near-identical copies. They are hard to spot in a long timeline because they sit next to dozens of similar frames.

A duplicate finder compares photos and **groups the ones that look the same**, so you decide once per group instead of scrolling forever.

## Step-by-step: remove duplicate photos

1. **Install a duplicate-finder app.** Download [FaceIdent](/faceident) from the App Store and allow access to your photos.
2. **Scan on-device.** The app compares your library on your iPhone and groups exact and near-identical shots. Nothing is uploaded.
3. **Review each group.** Open a group, keep the sharpest or best-framed shot, and select the others.
4. **Delete or hide.** Remove the extras to reclaim storage. Deleted photos stay in Recently Deleted for 30 days in case you change your mind.

## What to look for in a duplicate cleaner

| Feature | Why it matters |
| --- | --- |
| Near-duplicate detection | Catches burst shots and look-alikes, not just exact copies |
| Grouped review | Decide once per group instead of photo by photo |
| Keeps the best shot | Compare quality before you delete |
| Safe deletion | Extras go to Recently Deleted, recoverable for 30 days |
| On-device | Your library is never uploaded to compare it |

[FaceIdent](/faceident) does all of this on-device, alongside face and pet grouping and photo search — so cleanup is one part of keeping the whole library tidy.

## Common questions

**How do I find duplicate photos on iPhone?**
Use the Duplicates album in Photos, or an app like FaceIdent that groups exact and near-identical shots on-device.

**Can it catch near-identical shots?**
Yes. FaceIdent finds burst-style look-alikes that the stock album can miss.

**Are photos gone immediately?**
No. They move to Recently Deleted and can be restored for 30 days.
