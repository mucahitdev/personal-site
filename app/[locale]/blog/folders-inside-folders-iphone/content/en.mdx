# How to Put Folders Inside Folders on iPhone (Nested Folders)

**Quick answer:** You can't put a folder inside another folder on the iPhone Home Screen. iOS simply doesn't allow it. You can get real nested folders inside a widget with [WidgetLoft](/widgetloft) (formerly FolderMini): tap a folder tile and it opens right there in the widget, with a back tile to go up, as many levels deep as you want.

Dragged a folder onto another folder and watched it bounce back? You're not doing it wrong. Here is the workaround that works.

---

## Why iOS won't nest folders

Home Screen folders are one level deep: you can't drag a folder into another, and no setting changes that. So a "Work" folder can't contain a "Meetings" folder and a "Docs" folder, and you end up with either one giant folder or a Home Screen full of small ones.

A widget can do what a folder can't. In [WidgetLoft](/widgetloft), a group becomes a Home Screen widget, and a group can contain folders, which can contain more folders. Tapping a folder tile opens that folder in place, and a back tile takes you up one level.

## Step-by-step: create nested folders on iPhone

1. **Create a group and a folder.** Install [WidgetLoft](/widgetloft), create a group, open it and tap **New Folder**.
2. **Add apps and more folders.** Add apps to the folder. Tap **New Folder** again inside it to go another level deeper.
3. **Choose how the folder tile looks.** Show the folder as a 2×2 or 3×3 mini-grid of the apps inside, or give it its own icon from a photo or an SF Symbol.
4. **Place the widget.** Long-press the Home Screen, tap **+**, add the WidgetLoft widget and pick your group.
5. **Tune the back tile and auto-return.** In the **Back Button** editor pick a symbol (12 presets or any SF Symbol), a symbol color, a background color, background opacity from 0 to 100% and a size, or tap **Reset to Default**. Drag the back tile to any slot. Then go to **Settings → Widget → Auto-return to folder**.

With [WidgetLoft](/widgetloft), one widget can hold your whole day, sorted into folders, and every app tile still opens its app directly.

## Keep it tidy

- **Group by routine, not by app type.** A "Morning" folder with news, weather and your calendar is faster than a "Utilities" folder you have to search through.
- **Use auto-return.** With **Auto-return to folder**, the widget goes back to the top after you open an app, after a period of inactivity (10 to 60 seconds, 30 by default), or both. No more landing in a forgotten sub-folder.

## Common questions

**Can you put a folder inside a folder on iPhone?**
Not with regular Home Screen folders. A folder widget like WidgetLoft supports it: tap a folder tile and it opens inside the widget, with a back tile to go up.

**How many apps and sub-folders can a folder hold?**
Up to 100 apps and 20 sub-folders per folder, with no depth limit. The free version allows 6 groups and 21 library apps; WidgetLoft Pro removes those limits.

**Do nested folders work on the Lock Screen widget?**
No. The Lock Screen widget shows one row of top-level apps only. Nested folders work in the Home Screen widgets.
