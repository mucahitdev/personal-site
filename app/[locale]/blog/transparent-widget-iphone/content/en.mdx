# How to Make a Transparent Widget on iPhone (iOS 26 Liquid Glass)

**Quick answer:** On iOS 26 or later, open a group in [WidgetLoft](/widgetloft) (formerly FolderMini), tap **Edit Group** and choose the **Transparent** material. Add the regular **WidgetLoft** widget for a fully see-through look, or **WidgetLoft · Liquid Glass** for frosted glass. Either way your wallpaper shows through and only the app icons float on top.

Solid widget boxes can bury a wallpaper you picked with care. A transparent widget keeps the photo visible and still gives you one-tap access to your apps.

---

## What a transparent widget looks like

Most widgets sit on a solid or tinted card. A transparent widget drops that card, so the icons appear to sit right on your wallpaper, much like regular Home Screen icons, but arranged in a neat grid you control.

WidgetLoft offers two see-through styles for any group set to Transparent:

- **WidgetLoft** (the regular widget): the background disappears completely.
- **WidgetLoft · Liquid Glass**: the wallpaper shows through a soft frosted glass layer, which keeps icons readable on busy photos.

Both open apps directly when you tap a tile, just like any other [WidgetLoft](/widgetloft) widget.

## Step-by-step: make a transparent widget

1. **Open a group.** In WidgetLoft, go to the **Groups** tab and open the group you want on your Home Screen (or create a new one).
2. **Choose Transparent.** Tap **Edit Group** and set the material to **Transparent**. This option needs iOS 26 or later.
3. **Add the widget.** Long-press an empty spot on your Home Screen, open the widget gallery, search for WidgetLoft and pick **WidgetLoft** (fully see-through) or **WidgetLoft · Liquid Glass** (frosted). If it shows a different group, long-press the widget, tap **Edit Widget** and choose the one you made transparent.
4. **Pick the icon look.** For the Liquid Glass widget, go to **Settings → Widget → Dark Icon Appearance** and choose **Light** or **Dark** icons, whichever reads better on your wallpaper.
5. **Show app names if you like.** Turn on **App names** for the group to show each app's name under its icon.

## Transparent vs Liquid Glass vs Colour

| | Transparent | Liquid Glass | Colour |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Widget to add | WidgetLoft | WidgetLoft · Liquid Glass | WidgetLoft |
| Look | Fully see-through, icons on your wallpaper | Frosted glass over your wallpaper | Solid theme color, gradient and depth |
| Best wallpaper | Calm, simple backgrounds | Busy photos and bright patterns | Any, when you want the widget to stand out |
| Dark icons | Automatic, Light or Dark | Light or Dark only | Automatic, Light or Dark |
| iOS version | iOS 26 or later | iOS 26 or later | iOS 18 or later |

[WidgetLoft](/widgetloft) lets every group pick its own material, so you can mix a see-through group on one page with a colored one on another. For more layout inspiration, see our [iOS 27 Home Screen ideas](/blog/ios-27-home-screen-ideas).

## On iOS 18 to 25? Use Colour instead

Transparency is not available before iOS 26, but the **Colour** material works on iOS 18 and later. In **Edit Group** choose from 12 themes (Ember, Citrus, Bloom, Dusk, Tide, Aurora, Moss, Neon, Storm, Clay, Ink, Paper) or Custom, turn on **Gradient** and set its direction, then use the **Depth** slider to make the card flatter or richer. Pick a theme that matches your wallpaper and the widget blends in nicely.

## Common questions

**Can you make a widget transparent on iPhone?**
Yes, on iOS 26 or later. Set a WidgetLoft group to Transparent: the regular WidgetLoft widget becomes fully see-through, and the WidgetLoft · Liquid Glass widget gives a frosted glass look.

**Why can't I choose Transparent?**
Transparent needs iOS 26 or later. On iOS 18 to 25, use the Colour material with its 12 themes, Gradient and Depth.

**Does the Liquid Glass widget follow Dark Mode?**
Not automatically. Pick Light or Dark icons in Settings → Widget → Dark Icon Appearance. The regular WidgetLoft widget can follow the device with Automatic.
