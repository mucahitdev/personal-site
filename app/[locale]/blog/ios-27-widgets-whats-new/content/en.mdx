# What's New for Widgets in iOS 27 (and How to Use Them)

**Quick answer:** iOS 27, released September 14, 2026, brings three widget-related changes: an Extra Large widget size that fills a full Home Screen page, a Notification trigger for Shortcuts automations, and a compact clock mode on the Lock Screen. App launcher widgets like [WidgetLoft](/widgetloft) (formerly FolderMini) use the first two: one Extra Large widget holds up to 77 apps, and tiles can show a dot when an app gets a notification.

iOS 27 is not a widget overhaul, but its changes are practical. Here is what is new, what it means for app launcher widgets, and how to set it up.

---

## What changed for widgets in iOS 27

**Extra Large widget size.** The new size fills a full Home Screen page, a 4×6 grid area. Apple's Calendar, Photos, Music and Weather support it, and third-party apps can support it too.

**A Notification trigger in Shortcuts.** Shortcuts automations can now run when an app delivers a notification. That also lets widget apps react to your notifications.

**Compact clock on the Lock Screen.** Compact clock mode shows a smaller time at the top, next to the date and your widgets.

## What it means for app launcher widgets

[WidgetLoft](/widgetloft) turns groups of apps into Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets, and a tap on a tile opens the app directly. iOS 27 adds two things to that:

- **Extra Large holds up to 77 apps.** With small icons, one full-page widget fits 77 tiles (54 with medium icons, 40 with large, 24 with extra large). On iPad, the wide Extra Large holds up to 72.
- **Notification dots on tiles.** WidgetLoft uses the new Notification trigger to put a dot on an app's tile when that app gets a notification. It is a dot, not a count, and it clears when you open the app from the widget.

WidgetLoft 2.1.0 also adds two features that pair well with a big widget:

- **Dark icons for every app.** Popular apps come with ready-made dark icons and Apple's apps use Apple's real dark icons. Apps you add by App Store search now get a dark icon made right on your phone. Nothing is uploaded.
- **One-tap Dynamic Island.** With the Quick Launch bar turned on, apps in the Dynamic Island open in one tap, directly.

## Step-by-step: use the new iOS 27 widget features

1. **Update to iOS 27.** Open **Settings → General → Software Update** and install iOS 27.
2. **Add an Extra Large widget.** In [WidgetLoft](/widgetloft), create a group in the **Groups** tab and add your apps. Then long-press the Home Screen, tap **+**, search for WidgetLoft and choose the Extra Large size.
3. **Set up one notification dot.** In WidgetLoft, open **Settings → Notification Dots**. In Shortcuts, go to **Automation → Apps → Notification**, pick the app, keep Automation on and Notify off, and add the WidgetLoft action **Show Notification Dot** for the same app. It takes about a minute per app.
4. **Customize the Lock Screen.** Long-press the Lock Screen, tap **Customize**, switch to the compact clock, and add a WidgetLoft widget with one row of up to 4 apps (3 with large icons).

## iOS 27 widget changes at a glance

| iOS 27 change | What you can do with it |
| --- | --- |
| Extra Large widget size | Put up to 77 apps on one Home Screen page with WidgetLoft |
| Calendar, Photos, Music and Weather support Extra Large | Give a full page to the Apple app you check most |
| Shortcuts Notification trigger | Show a dot on a WidgetLoft tile when that app gets a notification |
| Lock Screen compact clock | Keep the time small next to your widgets, including a row of your top apps |

Apple's widgets mostly show information. [WidgetLoft](/widgetloft) uses the same new size and trigger to open your apps, and it still works on iOS 18 and later. For the full dot setup, read our guide to [notification badges on widgets](/blog/notification-badges-on-widgets-iphone).

## Common questions

**Which apps support the Extra Large widget in iOS 27?**
Calendar, Photos, Music and Weather, plus third-party apps. WidgetLoft fits up to 77 apps in one.

**Can iOS 27 widgets show notification counts?**
Not counts. WidgetLoft shows a dot, which clears when you open the app from the widget. Reading a notification only on the Lock Screen or Apple Watch does not clear it, and websites, Shortcuts tiles and contacts cannot have dots.

**Do I need iOS 27 to use WidgetLoft?**
No. WidgetLoft works on iOS 18 and later. Only the Extra Large size and notification dots need iOS 27.
