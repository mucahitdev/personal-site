# How to Search Your iPhone Photos by What's In Them (Objects & Natural Language)

**Quick answer:** To search your photos by content, use an app that recognizes the objects and scenes in each shot and lets you describe what you want in plain language. [FaceIdent](/faceident) indexes your library on-device and finds photos from descriptions like "dog on the beach" or "birthday cake" — no upload, no account.

You rarely remember the date a photo was taken, but you do remember what was *in* it. Content search matches that memory instead of a timeline.

---

## Three ways to search by content

A good on-device photo search combines a few signals:

- **Objects and scenes.** The app recognizes things like *beach*, *cake*, *car*, or *mountains* and lets you browse or search by them.
- **Natural language (semantic search).** You type a description — "sunset at the beach" — and it matches the *meaning*, not just exact tags.
- **Text inside photos.** Words on signs, screenshots, and receipts become searchable too.

[FaceIdent](/faceident) brings these together and runs all of it on your device.

## Step-by-step: search photos by what's in them

1. **Install a visual search app.** Download [FaceIdent](/faceident) from the App Store and allow access to your photos.
2. **Let it index on-device.** The app tags objects and scenes and prepares natural-language search on your iPhone. Nothing is uploaded.
3. **Search in plain language.** Type what you remember — "red car", "snow on the mountains", "coffee on a table".
4. **Refine.** Narrow results by a person's face or by text found inside the photo.

## What to look for in a photo search app

| Feature | Why it matters |
| --- | --- |
| Object & scene tags | Browse and search by what's actually in the shot |
| Natural-language search | Describe a photo instead of remembering keywords |
| Text (OCR) search | Find screenshots and receipts by the words inside them |
| People + content together | Combine a face with a scene to pinpoint one photo |
| On-device | Your library and index never leave the phone |

[FaceIdent](/faceident) covers all of these, and adds on-device face and pet grouping so people and content search work side by side.

## Is content search private?

It can be. When the indexing runs locally, the photos and everything derived from them stay on the device. [FaceIdent](/faceident) has no cloud and no account, so searching by content never uploads your library.

## Common questions

**Can I search iPhone photos by what's in them?**
Yes. FaceIdent tags objects and scenes and supports plain-language search, all on-device.

**What is semantic search?**
It matches the meaning of your words to the content of a photo, so a description finds the right shot even without exact keywords.

**Does it upload my photos?**
No. The index is built entirely on your iPhone.
