# How to Find All Photos of Your Pet on iPhone (Dog & Cat Grouping)

**Quick answer:** To find every photo of your dog or cat, use an app that recognizes pet faces and groups them automatically. [FaceIdent](/faceident) does this on-device — it detects your pet, gathers all of its photos into one group, and lets you open it in a tap. Nothing is uploaded and no account is needed.

Our pets are in hundreds of our photos, scattered across years. Grouping them turns "where's that one of the dog?" into a single tap.

## Yes, you can group pets like people

Face grouping is not just for people. The same on-device recognition that clusters your friends and family can recognize your **dog or cat** and gather every photo of them into one group. Instead of scrolling, you open your pet and see their whole story.

[FaceIdent](/faceident) groups pets and people side by side, entirely on your device.

## Step-by-step: find every photo of your pet

1. **Install a pet-grouping app.** Download [FaceIdent](/faceident) from the App Store and allow access to your photos.
2. **Let it scan on-device.** The app detects pet faces and creates a group for your dog or cat. Larger libraries take a little longer the first time.
3. **Open your pet.** Tap the group to see every photo your pet appears in.
4. **Name the group.** Give your pet a name so you can find their photos instantly.
5. **Merge if needed.** If your pet shows up in two groups, merge them so everything lives in one place.
